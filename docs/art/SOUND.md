# Sound

Every sound in the game is an ElevenLabs take made by `tools/art/sfx.mjs`
from a checked-in prompt (`audio/sfx/prompts.json`), recorded with its exact
request (`records.json`) and a verdict with a reason (`verdicts.json`), and
copied byte for byte to `public/sfx/`. Raw takes stay outside the repository
(`~/.studio-raw/border_marches/sfx`). Loudness is matched at playback by
`src/audio/levels.ts`, generated by `node tools/audio/sfx-levels.mjs write`.
Music is out of scope (the user adds it later).

- Sound pass 1 (bead bzc.4): 21 effects and the sound system.
- Sound pass 2 (bead bzc.27): 57 effects for shots, spell deliveries and
  impacts, battle details, map objects and the three factions; the event
  table below; throttles, delays, pitch jitter and a lower voice cap.

## Where sounds come from

There are two paths, and no moment uses both:

1. **Effect events** (`src/audio/fx.ts`). Every animation raises an event
   name; `FX_SOUNDS` maps it to a sound and `FX_OPTIONS` says how it plays.
   A battle beat raises its events together through `fxCues`, which plays
   each sound once, lets a spell shape's own sound replace the school's cast
   sound, and lets Frozen and Asleep replace the generic condition chime.
2. **Direct calls** (`playSound` with a cue from `src/audio/cues.ts`):
   battle hits and deaths (`battleCue`), map steps, pickups, level-ups, days
   and weeks, building and recruiting, victory and defeat, map object visits
   (`siteCue`), and the faction sounds (`recruitCue`, `victoryCue`,
   `townCue`). Events those moments also raise are left silent in the table.

## Mix

- **Loudness.** Every file plays at -18 dBFS RMS plus a per-sound trim
  (`TRIM_DB` in `tools/audio/sfx-levels.mjs`), peaks capped at -1 dBFS.
  Shots, deliveries and impacts sit 3 to 6 dB under; conditions, dialogs
  and ground effects 6 to 14 dB under; stings and fanfares at 0 to -1;
  town ambience at -8.
- **Throttles.** The same sound again within 45 ms is dropped; sounds that
  come in runs carry their own gap (ground 450-600 ms, conditions
  150-200 ms, shots 80 ms, impacts 100 ms, flags 300 ms).
- **Variation.** Hits, shots, pushes, landings, conditions and impacts get
  3-10% random pitch per play.
- **Voice cap.** At most 10 voices at once (was 12): a busy beat drops its
  extras instead of piling up.
- **Timing.** Spell impacts and landings are scheduled on the audio clock
  (`delay`, divided by the battle speed) so they land with the visual
  instead of on top of the cast.
- **Quiet by design.** Steps, ticks, turns, rounds, timeline moves, waits
  and defends make no sound of their own.
- **Never on its own.** No sound before the first gesture, in tests, in
  headless or automated browsers, or with `?mute=1`; nothing loops.

## Adding a sound

1. Add a prompt to `audio/sfx/prompts.json` (short, punchy, "short" and
   "no reverb" in the text; 0.5 to 1.2 s for effects).
2. `node tools/art/sfx.mjs generate --only <id>`; review with
   `node tools/audio/sfx-levels.mjs review` and a spectrogram; record
   `accept` or `reject` with a reason.
3. Add a mix trim to `TRIM_DB` if it should sit under or over the mix, then
   `node tools/audio/sfx-levels.mjs write`.
4. Map the event in `FX_SOUNDS` (and `FX_OPTIONS`) or call it from a cue,
   and update the tables here.
