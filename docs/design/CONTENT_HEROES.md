# Content: secondary skills, feats, vows and specialties

Status: content for v0.2 (bead `border_marches-bzc.6`, 2026-11-24).

Hero growth content for v0.2: 24 skills with three ranks, 28 feats, 12
vows and the specialty list. [RULES_V2.md](RULES_V2.md) sections 3.3 to
3.7 govern. Condition kinds are defined in [CONTENT_SPELLS.md](CONTENT_SPELLS.md). Classes,
Commands, stat patterns and named heroes belong to the faction documents.

## 1. Skills

A hero holds six skills. Each rank replaces the one before unless it says
"also". Percentages multiply into the Strike product (RULES_V2 1.8).
"Might" and "Magic" are suggested default class weights (0 to 10) for the
faction documents to adjust; the wheel factor of RULES_V2 3.3 multiplies
school skills.

feat fallback.

## 3. Vows

A vow is a feat with a restriction from the fixed list of RULES_V2 3.5.
It is offered only to a hero that already satisfies the restriction, and
at least one of the three feats in a draft is not a vow.

| Vow                             | Tags (offer weight)        | Restriction                                       | Rule                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vow of the Grave**            | Quietus                    | You can never cast Bloom.                         | Your Hush pages are not spent when cast on your own stacks or on remains.                          |
| **Vow of the Still Air**        | Covenant                   | You can never cast Storm.                         | Links your side makes are clockless.                                                               |
| **Vow of the Pyre**             | Stormcalling               | You can never cast Accord.                        | Your Storm pages deal no Harm to stacks fighting for your side.                                    |
| **Vow of the Opening**          | Horology, Ambush           | You cannot cast after round 3.                    | You may cast twice in round 1.                                                                     |
| **Vow of the Patient**          | Deep Wells, Bulwark        | You cannot cast in rounds 1 and 2.                | Your pages have +3 Power.                                                                          |
| **Vow of Silence**              | Deep Wells, Wayfinder      | You cannot cast in even-numbered rounds.          | Your pages cost half their mana, rounded up.                                                       |
| **Vow of the Borrowed Book**    | Packrat                    | You hold no school skill above rank 1.            | Your scrolls are not used up; each costs its page's mana and is spent once per battle like a page. |
| **Vow of the Unlettered**       | Onslaught, Bulwark, Banner | You hold no school skill at all.                  | Your stacks have +1 Speed and their Strikes are x 110%.                                            |

Restriction kinds used: a school never cast (3), rounds without casting
(3), a maximum school rank (2), an empty artifact slot kind (3), a barred

## 5. Decisions made here

| Decision                                                           | Why                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5 school, 10 army and 9 journey skills                             | The taxonomy's 24; hiring and guard parley share Silver Tongue               |
| Morale and Luck skills give +1, +2, +3                             | The flat budget of RULES_V2 3.4, and the full range of section 6 at rank 3   |
| Most feats use one condition kind                                  | Each new kind needs three users, and a feat is the cheapest place to put one |
| Vows trade a whole school, a slot kind or rounds for a rule switch | The restriction list of RULES_V2 3.5, one vow per build in the concept       |
| No Necromancy skill                                                | Concept decision 10                                                          |
